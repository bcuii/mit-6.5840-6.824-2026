The master carries only control information and metadata; the real data flows directly between workers. Notice that the master sits on the far left, and the thick line carrying 1TB never touches it — this is why a single master can serve 1700 workers.

Why a single master holds up

Drawn to scale for the 1TB sort job. All the control messages together take up less than one ten-thousandth of the data traffic.

data flow · the thick line 1 TB
metadata · through master ≈ 60 MB

What the master keeps is each task's state, plus the locations of the R intermediate files for every completed map task — that is where O(M × R) comes from: 15000 × 4000 = 60 million entries, about 1 byte per pair. Not one byte of data passes through it — metadata centralized, data distributed, the same design philosophy as GFS's master / chunkserver.
